## Summary

Spontus starts with Cal Poly club sports because the market is accessible, the teams have clear sponsorship pain, and sponsor relationships can be learned manually before platform automation. The near-term goal is to broker real conversations and deals, then convert the patterns into product.

## Current Wedge

- Geography: Cal Poly and San Luis Obispo.
- Team segment: college club sports with visible needs, events, travel, and sponsorable assets.
- Sponsor segment: local businesses, sports/nutrition/apparel/recovery brands, alumni-connected businesses, and brands already sponsoring comparable teams.

## Outreach Motion

1. Identify active club teams and their officers.
2. Interview teams to capture needs, assets, constraints, and sponsor history.
3. Identify sponsor leads tied to those needs.
4. Reach out manually and capture criteria.
5. Facilitate intros or listing-like opportunities.
6. Track outcome, objections, deliverables, and renewal potential.

## Near-Term Priorities

- Keep [Sponsor Pipeline](./sponsor-pipeline.md) current with lead stage, next action, owner, and follow-up date.
- Convert team interviews into structured research notes.
- Identify which sponsor categories match each team best.
- Draft reusable team profile and listing templates.
- Record every durable GTM decision in [Decision Log](../decision-log.md).
