## Pipeline Stages

- Lead identified
- Contact found
- Outreach sent
- Discovery call
- Offer discussed
- Deal agreed
- Deliverables in progress
- Proof submitted
- Renewal candidate

## Early Sponsor Sources

- Brands already sponsoring Cal Poly cycling, rugby, triathlon, and swim.
- Local San Luis Obispo businesses with student or sports relevance.
- Nutrition, beverage, apparel, recovery, and equipment brands.
- Alumni or team-member connections at relevant companies.

## Notes

Keep every sponsor conversation tied to a team, contact, proposed value, expected deliverables, and next follow-up date.
