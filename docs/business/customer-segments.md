# Customer Segments

## Initial Supply: Teams

College club sports teams with enough organization to deliver sponsor value but without dedicated sponsorship infrastructure.

### Best Early Teams

- Teams that compete regionally or nationally.
- Teams with visible jerseys, events, or social content.
- Teams with underfunded travel, gear, or event needs.
- Teams losing sponsor knowledge during officer turnover.

## Initial Demand: Sponsors

Brands that benefit from authentic grassroots sports exposure.

### Best Early Sponsors

- Nutrition, beverage, apparel, equipment, recovery, and local service brands.
- Local businesses near college towns.
- Brands already sponsoring one team and likely open to adjacent teams.
- Sponsors that value product sampling, event presence, jersey placement, and social proof.

## Buyer/User Split

Teams are daily users. Sponsors are buyers and campaign owners. Spontus needs to reduce work for both sides without hiding the relationship-building that makes sponsorships last.
