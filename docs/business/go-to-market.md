# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Phase 1: Learn Manually

Interview team officers, sponsorship coordinators, and sponsors. Focus on how deals are sourced, what sponsors provide, what teams promise, and where relationships break during officer turnover.

## Phase 2: Concierge Deals

Manually facilitate the first sponsorships. Use each deal to learn the real workflow before building marketplace automation.

## Phase 3: Cal Poly Network

Turn Cal Poly into the proof point: team profiles, sponsor/contact pipeline, repeatable deal templates, and proof-of-performance examples.

## Phase 4: Expand By Cluster

Expand to nearby schools and governing bodies once there is evidence that the playbook works beyond one campus.

## Positioning

Spontus is not a generic student organization sponsorship tool. It is the sponsorship layer for club sports teams and the sponsors that want authentic grassroots sports reach.
