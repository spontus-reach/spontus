## Summary

Audited Cal Poly club sports Instagram accounts to map the sponsorship landscape across all 30 teams. Found a clear pattern: 3-4 teams have figured out sponsorships through brute-force hustle. The other 26+ have zero sponsor presence. The gap is not willingness — it's infrastructure and know-how.

**Key stat for YC pitch:** 30 club sports at Cal Poly. Only 3-4 have any brand sponsorships. The other 26 are leaving money on the table.

---

## Tier 1: Teams With Multiple Sponsors (They Know How to Get Deals)

### Cycling

- **Sponsors on jersey:** Lezyne, Fluid, Maurten, GU, 100%, Schwalbe, Giro (7+ sponsors)
- **Sponsor types:** Mix of equipment (Lezyne, Schwalbe, Giro), nutrition (Maurten, GU, Fluid), and eyewear (100%)
- **Key detail:** Lezyne has an office in San Luis Obispo — this is a local relationship
- **Instagram:** Active, competes at national level
- **Assessment:** Gold standard for club team sponsorship at Cal Poly. Mirrors pro cycling culture where jersey sponsorship is deeply normalized. Great case study but may not need the platform urgently — they already have the relationships. However, they'd benefit from streamlined renewals and new brand discovery.

### Men's Rugby

- **Sponsors on jersey/shorts:** Celsius (shorts), Paladin, UCAN, Specialty Construction, BushTrack, and others visible on kit
- **Sponsor types:** Mix of national brands (Celsius) and local businesses (Specialty Construction)
- **Key detail:** Already has an NIL Club on nilclub.com where fans subscribe monthly and proceeds split among athletes. Already experimenting with monetization beyond traditional sponsorship.
- **Instagram:** Very active, high-level program
- **Assessment:** Most sophisticated sponsorship operation among Cal Poly clubs. Would be a power user of the platform. The mix of national and local sponsors proves both types of brands will sponsor club teams.

### Triathlon

- **Sponsors visible:** Roka (on athlete gear), Lezyne (confirmed — has dedicated page on Lezyne website)
- **Key detail:** Largest club sport on campus. Has leverage with brands due to size.
- **Instagram:** Active, competes at Wildflower and national events
- **Assessment:** Has some sponsorships but likely could have more given their size. Platform could help them scale up.

### Swim (Our Team)

- **Sponsors:** ~20 brands including Clif Bar, Arena, Lululemon (all product deals, no cash)
- **Key detail:** All deals handled by PR manager through cold email outreach
- **Assessment:** Proven demand. This is our founding case study.

## Tier 2: Equipment Partnerships Only (Sweet Spot for Platform)

These teams have brand logos on gear but these are likely bulk purchasing deals or equipment partnerships — NOT sponsorships where brands are paying or providing free product for exposure. The brand provides gear at a discount, the team buys it. Very different from a sponsorship.

### Men's Volleyball

- **Gear:** Under Armour branded jerseys
- **Other sponsors:** None visible
- **Competition level:** National level
- **Assessment:** Competing at nationals with zero commercial sponsors. Under Armour deal is likely a team purchase, not a sponsorship. High-visibility team that brands would want to sponsor.

### Men's Lacrosse

- **Gear:** Under Armour jerseys, Maverik (lacrosse brand) logo on shoulder
- **Other sponsors:** None visible
- **Competition level:** High level, regional/national
- **Assessment:** Same as volleyball. Equipment deal ≠ sponsorship. No commercial brand sponsors despite being a competitive, visible program.

### Men's Water Polo

- **Gear:** Turbo suits with CP logo
- **Other sponsors:** None visible
- **Competition level:** Highly regarded, placed high at regionals and nationals
- **Assessment:** Turbo is a water polo suit manufacturer — this is an equipment deal. No commercial sponsors despite national-level competition.

### Women's Water Polo

- **Gear:** Turbo suits
- **Other sponsors:** None visible
- **Competition level:** Regional and national events
- **Assessment:** Same as men's — equipment deal only, no commercial sponsors.

## Tier 3: Zero Sponsors, Zero Equipment Deals (Most Underserved)

These teams have Cal Poly branded or generic gear with no sponsor logos whatsoever. Some compete at high levels nationally. All are leaving money on the table.

### Baseball

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Instagram:** Very active
- **Competition level:** National level — impressive event schedule
- **Assessment:** High-profile team with strong Instagram presence. Zero sponsors. Prime candidate.

### Rowing

- **Sponsors:** None visible (Pocock Racing Shells on the boat is the manufacturer, not a sponsor)
- **Instagram:** Active, competed at 2026 Western Intercollegiate Championship
- **Competition level:** Regional championships
- **Assessment:** Active team with good content. No sponsors.

### Tennis

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Instagram:** Active
- **Competition level:** 2nd place at regionals in 2024
- **Assessment:** Competitive team, no sponsors. Custom Cal Poly shirts but no brand logos.

### Women's Ultimate Frisbee

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Competition level:** Competed at Dev Nationals
- **Assessment:** Active and competitive. No sponsors.

### Bowling

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Instagram:** Pretty active
- **Assessment:** Has uniforms but no sponsor logos.

### Hockey (Roller Hockey)

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Instagram:** Pretty active
- **Assessment:** Has uniforms, no sponsors.

### Water Ski

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Instagram:** Active but more recreational
- **Assessment:** Custom uniforms and wetsuits, no competitive events. More recreational — may be lower priority for brands but still potential for outdoor/lifestyle brands.

### Distance Club

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Instagram:** Active but more recreational
- **Assessment:** Recreational club, fewer competitions. Lower priority for sponsorship but could attract running shoe/nutrition brands.

### Ping Pong (Table Tennis)

- **Sponsors:** None visible
- **Assessment:** No obvious sponsors.
