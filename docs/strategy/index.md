## What Belongs Here

Use this folder for product thesis, competitive strategy, market wedge, team/sponsor definitions, audits, progress tracking, and decision context.

- Sponsor lead management and outbound process; use [Business](../business/index.md).
- Tooling and implementation notes; use [Engineering](../engineering/index.md).

Create a strategy doc when a thesis, wedge, positioning choice, or definition needs to guide multiple product or GTM decisions. Put implementation details in [Product](../product/index.md) or [Engineering](../engineering/index.md) instead.
