# Spontus HQ

> **Spontus** helps grassroots teams and sponsors find each other, structure credible deals, and maintain sponsorship relationships across seasons.

## What Spontus Is

Spontus is a two-sided sponsorship marketplace for college club sports and grassroots teams. Teams get verified profiles, reusable sponsorship assets, and sponsor discovery. Sponsors get a searchable network of legitimate teams, standardized comparison data, and a clearer path from interest to signed deal.

## Current Wedge

Cal Poly club sports are the first market. The research shows a sharp gap: a few teams have built sponsorship muscle through personal hustle, while most teams leave money and product support on the table because they lack contacts, process, and continuity.

## Operating Thesis

Use manual deal facilitation first. Learn the repeatable workflows, then turn those workflows into product: profiles, listings, applications, messaging, agreement templates, proof of performance, and renewals.
