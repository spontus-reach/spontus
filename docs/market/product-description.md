Spontus is a sponsorship operating system for grassroots teams and the brands that want to reach them.

## Core Problem

Teams need sponsorship support but usually rely on volunteer officers, cold outreach, personal connections, and scattered notes. Sponsors want credible grassroots opportunities, but comparing teams is slow and trust is weak.

## Product Promise

Spontus gives teams a verified profile, reusable sponsorship assets, deal templates, and sponsor discovery. Sponsors get searchable team profiles, standardized data, clear activation options, and a path to make deals without reading messy one-off emails.

## First Market

College club sports, starting with Cal Poly. This wedge is organized enough to deliver sponsorship value but under-served by existing sports sponsorship infrastructure.

## Primary Workflow

1. Team creates a verified profile.
2. Sponsor posts a listing or browses matching teams.
3. Team applies or sponsor reaches out.
4. Both sides use a structured agreement and deliverable checklist.
5. Team uploads proof of performance and renews the relationship next season.
