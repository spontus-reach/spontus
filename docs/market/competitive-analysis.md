# Competitive Analysis

## Core Differentiation

Spontus focuses on verified club sports teams, persistent team profiles, season-long sponsorship relationships, and structured deal follow-through.

## Closest Adjacent Competitors

- CampusLink: broad student organization sponsorship marketplace, more event-centric than team-relationship-centric.
- TeamSnap and TeamLinkt: youth sports infrastructure with sponsorship features, but not built for college club sports.
- OpenSponsorship and NIL platforms: individual athlete or influencer-style deals, not team sponsorship operations.
- SponsorUnited and Entyx: sponsorship intelligence or analytics, aimed higher upmarket.

## Strategic Takeaway

The market is active enough to validate demand, but no clear competitor owns college club sports sponsorships. The risk is that a generalist adds a sports vertical before Spontus builds category ownership.

## Defensive Wedge

Start with a narrow, credible network: Cal Poly club sports plus sponsors already active in the local and endurance-sports ecosystem.
