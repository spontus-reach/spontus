## Market Thesis

Grassroots sports sponsorship is fragmented. Demand exists on both sides, but the market lacks shared infrastructure for discovery, verification, deal structure, and continuity.

## Evidence From Cal Poly

A few teams have multiple sponsors because individual officers built relationships through hustle. Many comparable teams have no sponsors despite active competition schedules and visible audiences.

## Why College Club Sports First

- Teams are old enough to avoid many youth compliance issues.
- Teams are organized enough to deliver on sponsor promises.
- Teams are underfunded and motivated.
- Current sponsorship process is manual and fragile.

## Market Expansion Path

Cal Poly club sports to nearby California schools, then broader college club sports, then D3, NAIA, junior colleges, and selected youth or high-school programs once compliance is better understood.
