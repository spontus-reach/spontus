## When To Create A New Market Doc

Create a market doc when the topic is external evidence: competitors, category trends, market structure, funding landscape, or examples from adjacent sponsorship platforms. Promote implications into [Strategy](../strategy/index.md) or [Decision Log](../decision-log.md) when they change what Spontus will do.
